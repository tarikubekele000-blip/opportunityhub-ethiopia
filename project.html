OpportunityHub Ethiopia

A digital platform that helps Ethiopian students in Grades 9–12 discover trustworthy opportunities and take the next step toward applying.

The problem

Scholarships, competitions, camps, exchanges, and programs are announced across many Telegram channels, websites, social media pages, and school notices. Students often cannot tell which listings are current, whether they qualify, what documents they need, or where to apply. Students with limited access to networks and reliable internet are the most likely to miss out.

The solution

OpportunityHub Ethiopia gathers opportunities into one searchable website. Each listing follows the same format: organizer, category, target grade, delivery mode, eligibility, benefits, deadline, required materials, last-checked date, and an official application link. Students can filter, save, and track opportunities.

Objectives

  • Make discovery easier: students find opportunities using search, category, grade, and format filters.
  • Make information clearer: every listing shows eligibility, a deadline, and an official link.
  • Support action: students can save listings and track their application progress.
  • Support trust: listings are sourced, dated, and clearly labeled when unverified.
  • Deliver a working product: a deployed public demo with documentation.

Key features

  • Searchable directory with category, grade, and format filters, and sorting by deadline
  • Opportunity detail pages with eligibility, benefits, required materials, and the official link
  • Deadline labels such as "12 days left", "Closes today", and "Expired"
  • Saved opportunities and an application tracker (Interested, Preparing, Submitted, Closed)
  • Safety guidance and a correction route for reporting wrong information
  • Responsive layout, light and dark modes, and keyboard-friendly navigation

Limitations

  • The current listings are sample data and will be replaced with verified ones.
  • Saved items and tracker status are stored only in the visitor's own browser.
  • There are no accounts or admin dashboard yet. Both are planned.
  • The site does not claim to list every opportunity or to guarantee that every organizer is legitimate.

Project pitch: OpportunityHub Ethiopia reduces information barriers by putting verified opportunities, clear eligibility, and official sources in one organized place.
