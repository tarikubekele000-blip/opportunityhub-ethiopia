OpportunityHub Ethiopia

A digital platform that helps Ethiopian students in Grades 9–12 discover trustworthy opportunities and take the next step toward applying.

The problem

Scholarships, competitions, camps, exchanges, and programs are announced across many Telegram channels, websites, social media pages, and school notices. Students often cannot tell which listings are current, whether they qualify, what documents they need, or where to apply. Students with limited access to networks and reliable internet are the most likely to miss out.

The solution

OpportunityHub Ethiopia gathers opportunities into one searchable website. Each listing follows the same format: organizer, category, target grade, delivery format, eligibility, benefits, deadline, required materials, last-checked date, and a link to the official application page. Students can search, filter, save, and track opportunities.

Key features

  • Searchable directory with category, grade, and format filters, and sorting by deadline
  • Opportunity pages with eligibility, benefits, required materials, and the official link
  • Deadline labels such as "12 days left", "Closes today", and "Expired"
  • Clear notes when a deadline has not been announced yet, instead of guessed dates
  • Saved opportunities and an application tracker (Interested, Preparing, Submitted, Closed)
  • A similar-opportunities section and a copy-link button on each listing
  • Safety guidance and a route for reporting corrections
  • Responsive layout, light and dark modes, and keyboard-friendly navigation

The pages read the listings file, show the results, and send the student to the organizer's official page to apply. Saved items and tracker status are stored only in the student's browser.

How listings are verified

  • Each opportunity is checked on the organizer's own website, not only on news or social media posts.
  • The deadline, eligibility, benefits, and required materials are copied from the official page and written in plain language.
  • If the organizer has not announced a date, the listing says so and explains what is known, instead of guessing.
  • Each listing shows the date it was last checked, and expired listings are marked automatically.
  • Students are always sent to the official page to apply, and the site never collects applications or fees.

Design decisions

  • Trust first: every listing shows its source, its last-checked date, and a reminder to confirm on the official page.
  • Privacy by design: the site has no accounts, no tracking, and no forms that collect personal data. Student choices stay on their own device.
  • Accessibility: a skip link, labeled form fields, visible keyboard focus, screen-reader announcements for search results, and respect for reduced-motion settings.
  • Works for everyone: a mobile-first layout, light and dark themes, and no heavy downloads, so pages load quickly on slow connections.

Scope

  • The site presents a carefully checked selection of opportunities. It does not claim to list every opportunity or to guarantee that any organizer is legitimate.
  • Deadlines and rules can change, so every listing points students to the official page for final confirmation.
