# Requirements

## Who is it for?
- Students in Grades 9-12 who want to find opportunities
- Admins who add and update the opportunities

## What students can do
- Browse a list of opportunities
- Search by keyword
- Filter by category and grade
- Open one opportunity to see details, eligibility, deadline, and official link
- Create an account, save opportunities, and track application status

## What admins can do
- Log in
- Add, edit, and remove opportunities
- Publish or unpublish an opportunity

## Each opportunity shows
Title, organizer, category, grade, location, eligibility, benefits,
deadline, official link, and the date it was last checked.

## Categories
Scholarship, competition, camp, exchange, technology, course,
internship, volunteering.

## Rules
- Every opportunity must link to its official source.
- Never invent deadlines or details.
- Mark anything unverified clearly.

## Not in version 1
Recommendations, automatic data collection, mobile app, notifications.
