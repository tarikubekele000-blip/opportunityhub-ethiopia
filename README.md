# OpportunityHub Ethiopia

live at https://tarikubekele000-blip.github.io/opportunityhub-ethiopia/


A website that helps Ethiopian Grade 9-12 students find scholarships,
competitions, camps, and other programs in one place.

## The Problem
Opportunities are scattered across Telegram channels, websites, and social
media, so students often miss them or can't tell if they are real.

## The Solution
One searchable website where every opportunity shows:
- who is offering it
- who can apply
- the deadline
- a link to the official page

Students can also save opportunities and track their applications.

## Tech Stack
React, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL

## Status
In planning. Features are being added step by step.
