About OpportunityHub Ethiopia

Many students miss useful opportunities because the information is spread across Telegram channels, websites, and social media. This site puts scholarships, competitions, camps, and programs for Grades 9–12 in one searchable place.

How listings are checked

  • Every listing links to the organizer's official page.
  • Each listing shows the date it was last checked.
  • Deadlines and eligibility are rechecked regularly, and expired listings are marked.
  • Anything unverified or incomplete is clearly labeled. We never invent deadlines or benefits.

This site does not list every opportunity and cannot guarantee that every organizer is legitimate. Always do your own checks.

Staying safe when you apply

  • Apply only through the organizer's official website.
  • Check the web address carefully before entering any information.
  • Be cautious of anyone who asks for money just to consider your application.
  • Never share your passwords or one-time codes.
  • If you are unsure, ask a teacher, parent, or counselor before applying.

Report a correction

Found a wrong deadline, a broken link, or a suspicious listing? Please tell us and we will fix or remove it.
